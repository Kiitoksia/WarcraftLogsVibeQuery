Ula'tek Wave Patterns

Every wave volley from report 3t7kArhXbNVPKzcq, turned so Ula'tek is always at the top of the map. Seen from the boss, the waves follow a short script that depends on which way she moves after the opening: pick the tab for this pull, then each combination shows where the waves start, where they go, where people got hit, and the spot that stayed clean.

Reading the maps

Directions

  • Boss's left / right are as Ula'tek faces into the platform (her left is the right of the map).
  • Near is the half by the boss, far the half opposite her.
  • Mid is 15–35 yd from the centre, edge beyond that.

Which way did she move?

Ula'tek starts on the north edge and moves to the west or east edge after a minute or so. Left and right are as the raid sees it, facing her at the start.

How this was worked out. Gore Rattle waves travel in a straight line along the way the Rattle faced when it cast (86% of hits fall within 15° of that line), at about 14 yd per second, about 11 yd either side. Hits are where players stood when Caustic Waves hit them within 10 seconds of a volley; "not hit" is where everyone else stood.

The boss's sweep isn't in the log. Its side is read from the raid: in the 3 seconds after every boss cast, everyone sidesteps about 12 yd to one side. The covered half is drawn as 5 parallel waves, 10 yd apart, starting 4 yd beside her; that spacing comes from the raid's description, not the log.

"Clean" only covers places someone actually stood. A safe spot here means it worked every time people stood there, which is good evidence but not a guarantee.
